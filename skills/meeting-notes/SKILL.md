# Meeting notes

Output exactly these sections:

**Summary**: 3 sentences maximum. What the meeting was for and what came out of it.

**Decisions**: bullet list. Only things that were actually agreed, not ideas.

**Action items**: table with columns Task | Owner | Due date. Use names from the transcript. If owner or date was not said, write "unassigned" / "no date" instead of inventing one.

**Open questions**: things raised but not resolved.

Rules:
- Do not add information that is not in the transcript.
- Keep quotes only when exact wording matters (numbers, commitments).
- Write in the language of the transcript.

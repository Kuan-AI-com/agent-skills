# SEO article writer

1. Identify the search intent behind the keyword: learn, compare, buy or fix. The article format follows the intent (guide, comparison, list, tutorial).
2. Outline first and show it: H1 with the keyword, H2s that answer the questions people search next, a FAQ with 3-5 real questions.
3. Write:
   - first paragraph answers the query directly in 2-3 sentences
   - short paragraphs, concrete examples, numbers with sources
   - one table or checklist where it helps scanning
   - no filler ("In today's fast-paced world")
4. Add a meta title (under 60 characters) and meta description (under 155) that include the keyword naturally.
5. Suggest 2-3 internal links and the anchor text for each.
6. Flag any claim that needs a source you could not verify.

# Landing page copy

Full landing page copy built from your customer's problem and only the proof you give it: no hype, no invented numbers.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

Write landing page copy for {{PRODUCT: what it is and does}}.

Customer: {{AUDIENCE}}
Main problem it solves: {{PROBLEM}}
What they use today instead: {{ALTERNATIVE}}
Customer quotes, reviews or interview notes (optional): {{CUSTOMER WORDS}}
Proof I can show: numbers, testimonials, logos (optional): {{PROOF}}
Price (optional): {{PRICE}}

Sections:
1. Hero: headline (max 10 words, the customer's outcome), subheadline (how, max 25 words), CTA button (2-4 words).
2. Problem: 3 pains. Use the customer words if given; otherwise write them plainly and mark them [validate].
3. How it works: 3 steps.
4. Benefits: 3-4, each "outcome, because feature".
5. Why switch from {{ALTERNATIVE}}: 2-3 honest differences.
6. Social proof: only what is in PROOF; otherwise [placeholder: testimonial from ...].
7. Pricing summary (or [placeholder] if no price).
8. FAQ: the 5 objections most likely to stop a purchase, answered honestly.
9. Final CTA.

Rules: plain words a customer would say. No "revolutionary", "seamless", "unlock", "supercharge", "game-changer", "all-in-one". No numbers, customers or results I did not give you.
Then: 3 alternative hero headlines, each from a different angle.
Before finishing, check every claim against my inputs and remove anything you cannot trace back to them.

Sections:
1. Hero: headline (under 10 words, outcome for the customer), subheadline (how), CTA button text.
2. Problem: 3 pains in the customer's words.
3. How it works: 3 steps.
4. Benefits: 3-4, each as outcome + feature that makes it possible.
5. Social proof (only what I gave you; leave placeholders otherwise).
6. Pricing summary.
7. FAQ: the 5 objections that stop people buying, answered honestly.
8. Final CTA.

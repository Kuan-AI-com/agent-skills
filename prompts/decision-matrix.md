# Decision matrix

Compares options on weighted criteria and checks the decision with a pre-mortem.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

I need to decide: {{DECISION}}

Options: {{OPTION A, OPTION B, ...}}
What matters to me: {{CRITERIA}}

1. Turn my criteria into 4-6 measurable ones and propose weights (ask me to adjust them).
2. Score each option 1-5 per criterion, with one line of reasoning per score.
3. Show the weighted table and the total.
4. Pre-mortem: imagine it is a year later and the winning option failed. What went wrong?
5. Is the decision reversible? If yes, suggest a cheap way to test it before committing.

Point out if my criteria are missing something important.

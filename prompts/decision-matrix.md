# Decision matrix

Compares options on weighted criteria, tests whether the winner survives other weights, and runs a pre-mortem before you commit.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

I need to decide: {{DECISION}}

Options: {{OPTIONS: A, B, C...}}
What matters to me: {{CRITERIA}}
Facts I know about each option (optional): {{FACTS}}
Deadline or constraints (optional): {{CONSTRAINTS}}

1. Turn my criteria into 4-6 concrete, comparable criteria. Propose weights that add up to 100%, state them, and continue (I will adjust later).
2. Score each option 1-5 per criterion with one line of reasoning. If you lack the facts, write "?" and the question I must answer, instead of guessing.
3. Weighted table: option | each criterion | total.
4. Sensitivity: would the winner change if the top criterion's weight moved by 10 points, or if each "?" went to its worst case? Say which.
5. Pre-mortem: a year from now the winner failed. The 3 most likely reasons, and an early warning sign for each.
6. Reversibility: if the decision is reversible, a cheap test to run before committing.
7. Anything important missing from my criteria.

End with a one-line recommendation and how confident you are (low / medium / high), with the reason.

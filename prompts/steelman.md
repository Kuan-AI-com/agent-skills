# Steelman both sides

Lays out the strongest case for and against a question, the shared facts and the real crux, with what evidence would settle it.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

Question or decision: {{QUESTION}}
My context: {{CONTEXT: who decides, constraints, what is at stake}} (optional)

Act as a fair moderator, not an advocate.

1. **Strongest case FOR**: as its smartest supporter would argue it. 3-5 points, best evidence first.
2. **Strongest case AGAINST**: same standard. No straw men on either side.
3. **Common ground**: facts both sides accept.
4. **The crux**: the 1-2 questions where they really disagree, and what evidence or test would settle each.
5. **What would change my mind**: for each side, the one fact that would most weaken it.

Rules:
- Label each key claim: [established], [contested] or [my inference]. Do not invent studies, statistics or quotes; describe the kind of evidence instead if you are unsure of specifics.
- Keep both sides about the same length.
- Do not give your own verdict. I will ask for it separately.

# Steelman both sides

Gets the strongest version of each side of a debate before you decide.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

Topic: {{QUESTION OR DECISION}}

1. Give the strongest possible case FOR, as its smartest supporter would argue it. Use the best evidence, not straw men.
2. Give the strongest possible case AGAINST, the same way.
3. List the facts both sides agree on.
4. Name the real crux: the one or two questions where they disagree, and what evidence would settle each.
5. Only then, if I ask, give your own assessment and your confidence.

Mark anything you are unsure is accurate.

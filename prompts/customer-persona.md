# Customer persona from real data

Builds a persona from the reviews, interviews or tickets you paste, with quotes as evidence and 'unknown' instead of stereotypes.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

Build a customer persona for {{PRODUCT}} from the real customer data below (reviews, interview notes, support tickets, survey answers).

For each persona, include:
- Who they are (role, situation), only as far as the data shows
- The job they hire the product for
- Triggers: what made them start looking
- Pains, in their exact words (quote the data)
- Objections or doubts before buying
- Words they use, and words they never use
- Where they look for advice and who they trust (only if the data says so)

Rules:
- Every point cites evidence: a short quote or a source reference, plus how many sources say something similar.
- Mark a point "weak signal" if only one source supports it.
- If the data shows 2 or more distinct groups, make one persona each and say what separates them.
- Do not invent age, gender, income, names or photos-style details. Write "unknown" for anything the data does not show.

Output: one section per persona with the headings above, then a short list of the 3 questions to ask in the next customer interviews to fill the biggest gaps.

# Negotiation reply

Answers an offer, discount request or late payer firmly, with a trade for every concession and your walk-away point kept hidden.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

I am negotiating with {{WHO: client, supplier, employer...}} about {{WHAT}}.

Their message:
"""
{{THEIR MESSAGE}}
"""

What I want: {{GOAL}}
My walk-away point: {{MINIMUM}}
What I can give in return (optional): {{CONCESSIONS}}
My alternative if this fails (optional): {{ALTERNATIVE}}
Channel and relationship: {{CHANNEL: email / chat / call}}, {{RELATIONSHIP: one-off / long-term / key account}}

1. What they probably want, reading between the lines (2-3 bullets).
2. My best strategy and why, in 3 lines max.
3. A reply draft: friendly, firm, specific numbers, every concession traded ("if you..., I can..."). Max 120 words for email or chat; talking points for a call.
4. Their 2 most likely counter-moves and my answer to each, one line each.

Rules:
- Never reveal or hint at my walk-away point or my alternative.
- No invented facts: no fake competing offers, deadlines or costs.
- No apologizing for the price and no ultimatums unless I ask for one.

# SEO topic cluster

Plans a pillar page and 12-15 supporting articles by search intent, without made-up search volumes, ready to check in your keyword tool.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

Plan an SEO topic cluster for {{SITE: what the site is and who it serves}} that wants to rank for {{MAIN TOPIC}}.
What the business sells or wants readers to do: {{GOAL}}
Keyword data from a tool like Ahrefs/Semrush/Search Console (optional): {{KEYWORD DATA}}
Pages that already exist (optional): {{EXISTING PAGES}}

You cannot see live search data. Do not state search volumes, difficulty scores or traffic numbers unless they are in KEYWORD DATA.

1. Pillar page: title, the search intent it serves, and the H2 outline.
2. 12-15 supporting articles as a table: # | target keyword | intent (learn / compare / buy / fix) | title | pillar H2 it links to | why it is a separate article.
3. Cannibalization check: merge any two articles that would compete for the same query and say which ones you merged.
4. Publishing order: start with the most specific, long-tail and bottom-of-funnel topics, then broader ones. Explain the logic, without claiming difficulty data.
5. Internal linking rules: pillar to articles, articles to pillar, and between articles.
6. A list of the keywords to verify first in a keyword tool, and what result would make you drop or change an article.

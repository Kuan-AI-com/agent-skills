# Cold email that gets replies

Writes a short cold email built on real research about the prospect, with a low-effort ask and two follow-ups that each add something new.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

Write a cold email from me to a prospect.

Me: {{SENDER: name, role, company in one line}}
Prospect: {{PROSPECT: role and company}}
Why them, specifically: {{RESEARCH: something real and recent about them}}
What I offer: {{OFFER: the outcome, not the feature list}}
Proof: {{PROOF: a result for a similar client}} (optional; if empty, use no proof)

Rules:
- Email under 90 words. Subject under 6 words, lowercase, no clickbait or fake "re:".
- First sentence is about them, built on the research. Never invent facts, results or mutual connections.
- One low-effort ask: a yes/no question, not "a 30-minute call".
- No "I hope you're well", "I came across your profile", "quick question", buzzwords or exclamation marks.
- Follow-up 1 (day 3) and follow-up 2 (day 7): under 50 words each, each adds one new thing (an insight, a resource, a different angle). No "just bumping this".

Check word counts before answering.

Output exactly:
**Subject:** ...
**Email:** ...
**Follow-up 1 (day 3):** ...
**Follow-up 2 (day 7):** ...

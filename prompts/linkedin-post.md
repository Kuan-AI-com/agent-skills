# LinkedIn post from an experience

Turns something you did or learned into a specific, non-cringe LinkedIn post using only your real details, with 3 hooks to pick from.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

You write LinkedIn posts for practitioners who hate corporate fluff. Write a post from my experience below.

What happened: {{STORY: what I did, what went wrong or right}}
Concrete details: {{DETAILS: numbers, names of tools, dates, a quote; the more the better}}
The one lesson: {{LESSON}}
Audience: {{AUDIENCE: e.g. founders, backend developers}}

Rules:
- Use only facts from my input. Never invent numbers, results, clients or quotes; if a detail would help but is missing, write [ADD: ...].
- Line 1: a concrete hook (a number, a mistake, a result). No "I'm thrilled to announce", "Here's what I learned", "Let that sink in".
- 3-5 short paragraphs (1-3 sentences each), not one sentence per line.
- One lesson, stated plainly. End with a specific question experienced people can answer (not "Agree?").
- 120-200 words. At most 2 hashtags, at the end. No emoji bullets.

Output exactly:
1. Three alternative first lines.
2. The full post using the strongest one.
3. Word count.

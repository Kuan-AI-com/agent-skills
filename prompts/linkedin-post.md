Audience: {{WHO: e.g. founders, backend developers, recruiters}}.

Rules:
- First line: a specific, concrete hook (a number, a mistake, a result). No "I'm thrilled to announce".
- Tell what happened in 3-5 short paragraphs, with one real detail that only I would know.
- One lesson, stated plainly. Not five.
- End with a question that invites experienced people to answer.
- Under 200 words. No hashtag walls (2 max). No emojis as bullet points.

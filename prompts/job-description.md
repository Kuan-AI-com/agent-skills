Details: {{LOCATION / REMOTE, SALARY RANGE, TEAM, WHO THEY REPORT TO}}

Structure:
- 2 sentences on the company, no buzzwords.
- What you will do in the first 3 months (specific projects).
- What success looks like after a year.
- Must-haves: maximum 5, only what is truly required.
- Nice-to-haves: maximum 3.
- Salary range and benefits.
- How the hiring process works (steps and timing).

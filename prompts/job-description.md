# Job description people finish reading

A job post about the real work, first-90-day projects and honest requirements, with salary shown and no clichés or invented perks.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

Write a job description for {{ROLE}} at {{COMPANY: what it does, size, stage}}.

Location / remote: {{LOCATION}}
Salary range: {{SALARY}}
Team and who they report to: {{TEAM}}
Real projects for the first months: {{PROJECTS}}
Requirements I have in mind: {{REQUIREMENTS}}
Benefits and hiring steps (optional): {{BENEFITS AND PROCESS}}

Structure:
- 2 sentences on the company: what it does and why this role exists now.
- What you will do in the first 3 months: specific projects.
- What success looks like after a year.
- Must-haves: max 5. Nice-to-haves: max 3.
- Salary range and benefits.
- Hiring process: steps and timing.

Rules:
- 350-600 words. Plain, inclusive language; address the reader as "you".
- No "rockstar", "ninja", "fast-paced", "wear many hats", "work hard play hard", "we are a family".
- Do not invent benefits, perks, salary or process steps; leave [placeholder] where I gave nothing.
- After the post, list any of my requirements that may filter out good candidates without reason (degrees, years of experience, "native speaker") and suggest a fairer version.

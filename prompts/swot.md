# Honest SWOT

A SWOT analysis that leads to actions instead of a pretty grid.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

Do a SWOT analysis for {{BUSINESS / PROJECT}}.

Context: {{MARKET, TEAM, NUMBERS, GOALS}}

- 3-5 items per quadrant, each specific to this business (no generic "strong team").
- Mark which items are facts from my context and which are your assumptions.
- Then the useful part:
  - SO: how to use strengths to grab opportunities
  - WT: which weakness + threat combination could kill the business, and the cheapest way to defend
- End with the 3 actions to take this quarter, in priority order.

# Honest SWOT

A SWOT ranked by impact, with facts separated from assumptions, that ends in the 3 actions to take this quarter.

Replace the {{PLACEHOLDERS}} before sending.

---

Do a SWOT analysis for {{BUSINESS OR PROJECT}}.

Context: {{MARKET, CUSTOMERS, TEAM, NUMBERS, GOALS}}
Main competitors (optional): {{COMPETITORS}}

Rules:
- 3-5 items per quadrant, each specific to this business. Nothing that would fit any company ("strong team", "growing market").
- Tag each item [fact] if it comes from my context or [assumption] if it is your inference.
- Rank items inside each quadrant by impact on the goal.
- Do not invent market sizes, growth rates or competitor numbers.

Output:
1. The four quadrants as lists.
2. Strategy combinations, 1-2 lines each:
   - SO: use a strength to grab an opportunity
   - WO: fix a weakness that blocks an opportunity
   - ST: use a strength to blunt a threat
   - WT: the weakness + threat combination that could kill the business, and the cheapest defense
3. The 3 actions for this quarter, in priority order, each with an owner role and a sign of success.
4. The assumptions that matter most and how to check them quickly.
